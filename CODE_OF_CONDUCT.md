# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent,
give actionable technical feedback, and keep security reports private until a
fix or coordinated disclosure is agreed. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, credential abuse, and deliberate disclosure of private traffic or
personal data are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate
these expectations. For security-sensitive concerns, use the process in
`SECURITY.md` rather than a public issue.
